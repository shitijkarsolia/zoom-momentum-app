# Zoom Momentum — Presentation Script

~2-3 minutes, conversational tone.

---

## Opening

"Hey everyone, I'm [Name]. I'm going to show you Zoom Momentum — something I built for the Zoom Apps Fellowship."

---

## The Problem

"So here's the thing about Zoom lectures. You're listening, you miss one term, you space out for 30 seconds, and suddenly you're lost. There's no rewind button. You can't raise your hand and say 'what did I miss?' And on the other side — the professor is talking into a void. They have no idea if anyone's actually following along."

---

## What It Is

"Zoom Momentum sits right inside the Zoom meeting. While the professor lectures, it's listening — it pulls out the key topics, defines technical terms, and builds a live timeline of everything being discussed. Students see all of this in real time."

---

## Key Features (keep it quick)

"A few things it does:

- It translates the entire transcript live into 6 languages — so international students can follow along in their own language.

- The professor can launch quick polls to check if people are keeping up — the AI writes the questions based on what was just said.

- There's a trivia game mode where students compete on a leaderboard — makes review actually engaging.

- And at the end of class, every student gets a personalized study guide based on what they bookmarked during the lecture."

---

## Who It's For

"It's for any class that happens on Zoom — especially large lectures where the professor can't check in with everyone individually, and especially for international students who are trying to follow along in a second language."

---

## Close

"Everything happens inside the meeting — no extra apps, no extra setup. The professor gets feedback, students get support, and everyone walks away with something useful. Let me show you the demo."
