What It Does

A live classroom loop inside Zoom.

Zoom Momentum gives the host real-time teaching controls and gives students a structured way to recover when attention breaks.

01

Professor's Pulse

Built prototype

AI-generated comprehension checks inside the live lecture.

Professors generate, preview, edit, launch, and end short polls without leaving the meeting flow. Students answer in real time and results come back immediately.

02

Warm-Up Arena

Live in dev

A timed review game to activate attention before the lecture settles.

Hosts launch a fast quiz round, students answer against the clock, and the app keeps score with leaderboard-style energy rather than traditional LMS friction.

03

Live Anchor

RTMS-ready concept

A rolling topic timeline and glossary built from transcript context.

The app polls the lecture transcript, detects topic changes, extracts vocabulary, and keeps students oriented even when the class moves quickly.

04

Recovery Agent

Post-class prototype

Confusion is captured in class and transformed into post-class repair.

Students bookmark confusing moments privately. After class, those moments can become a structured recovery pack with explanations, practice, and follow-up resources.
