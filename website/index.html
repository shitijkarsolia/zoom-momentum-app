What it does

A live classroom loop inside Zoom.

Four tools built on the same engine: the live lecture transcript. Professors get to check understanding while there's still time to do something about it, and students keep their bearings during class, then repair the gaps after.

01

Professor's Pulse

Live in the demo

AI-generated comprehension checks, mid-lecture.

One click drafts a poll from the last few minutes of lecture. The professor edits it, launches it, and watches the answers tally live. It reads the whole room in under a minute, and nobody has to grade a thing.

02

Warm-Up Arena

Live in the demo

A timed trivia game that wakes the room up.

AI writes review questions from the lecture, students race a countdown, and faster correct answers score higher on a live leaderboard. Closer to a game show than an LMS quiz.

03

Live Anchor

Live in the demo

A topic timeline and glossary that write themselves.

Momentum listens to the live transcript, marks every topic change, and defines new terms the moment they're said, in any of six languages. Look up from your notes, join late, or lose the thread entirely, and you still know exactly where the class is.

04

Recovery Agent

Live in the demo

Confusion captured in class, repaired after.

Students bookmark hard moments privately, and when the professor says "this will be on the exam," Momentum bookmarks it for them. After class, every bookmark turns into a recovery pack with plain-language explanations, practice prompts, and notes to download.

Fellowship project

Made through the Zoom Fellowship at ASU Next Lab.

Built by Shitij Mathur

Built after one too many lectures where half the room had quietly fallen behind.

Momentum started as a Zoom Fellowship project and grew into a working app: live comprehension checks for professors, a running map of the lecture for students, and recovery material for everything that didn't land. All of it lives inside the meeting, because that's where the class actually happens.
